Function Reference: parts.material

parts: M = parts.material ()

parts: M = parts.material (NAME)

parts: M = parts.material (NAME, SIZE)

Properties of the materials parts are made of.

M = parts.material () returns every material in the catalogue as a struct array. M = parts.material (NAME) returns the one named, such as 'C45'. For a metal, whose strength depends on the size of the section, M = parts.material (NAME, SIZE) returns the strength for a section SIZE millimetres across, the diameter of a bar or the thickness of a plate; without SIZE it returns the smallest section’s.

Each material is a struct with these fields, stresses in megapascals:

namethe name it is asked for by
class'metal', 'machined polymer' or 'printed polymer'
EYoung’s modulus
nuPoisson’s ratio
Rpyield strength, 0.2 per cent proof stress or tensile stress at yield; empty where the source gives none
Rmtensile strength; empty where the source gives none
Aelongation at break, per cent; empty where the source gives none
rhodensity, kilograms per cubic metre
sizesfor a metal, the rows [up to, Rp, Rm, A] the strength is tabulated in; empty for a polymer
sourcewhere the values come from

Values are minimums where the source states minimums. A printed polymer carries the weaker of the orientations its datasheet gives, since a printed part is loaded across its layers somewhere.

'PETG'Prusament PETG, technical datasheet 1.1 (2022), ISO 527-1 on printed specimens.
'PETG-CF'Prusament PETG Carbon Fiber, technical datasheet 1.0 (2022), ISO 527-1 on printed specimens.
'PA6-CF'Polymaker Fiberon PA6-CF20, technical datasheet 1.1 (2024): wet, after 48 hours in water at 60 degrees Celsius, 5.3 per cent moisture, loaded across the layers (Z). It gives no yield strength.
'Ertalon 6 SA'Machined PA6 stock: conditioned at 23 degrees Celsius and 50 per cent humidity, from an older undated product sheet that calls these values mostly derived from literature; density from the Mitsubishi Chemical Advanced Materials sheet of 2024. It gives no tensile strength.

No datasheet of the polymers, nor of CuSn12, gives a Poisson’s ratio: the nylons take 0.40, conditioned polyamide in Bayer’s design guide Engineering Polymers: Part and Mold Design (2000), Table 3-1; the PETGs 0.40, DuPont’s rule for plastics of modulus 500 to 2500 MPa in General Design Principles, Module I; CuSn12 0.35, as tabulated for centrifugally cast CuSn12 by Gruender et al., IOP Conf. Ser.: Mater. Sci. Eng. 1190 (2021) 012006, Table 3.
