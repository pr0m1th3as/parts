Function Reference: parts.bearing.deepgroove

parts: B = parts.bearing.deepgroove ('data')

parts: B = parts.bearing.deepgroove ('data', DESIGNATION)

parts: S = parts.bearing.deepgroove ('solid', DESIGNATION)

Single row deep groove ball bearings from the catalogue.

B = parts.bearing.deepgroove ('data') returns every bearing in the catalogue as a table, one row per bearing, sorted by bore, then outside diameter, then width. B = parts.bearing.deepgroove ('data', DESIGNATION) returns the one bearing named, such as '6205' or '61930 MA'. Nothing is built.

S = parts.bearing.deepgroove ('solid', DESIGNATION) builds the bearing as a solid.Shape, its axis on the z axis, one face on the xy plane and the other at z = B: the inner and the outer ring, each with its raceway, and the balls between them, each a solid of its own; the cage is left out. The catalogue gives no internal geometry, so the shape takes the proportions of the type: balls of diameter Dw = min (0.3*(D - d), 0.8*B) on the pitch circle (d + D)/2, as many as fill 60 per cent of it; each ring stops 0.3*Dw short of the pitch circle, with a groove of radius 0.52*Dw, so that the balls run free; the outer corners of the rings are rounded to r. It shows the bearing; it does not stand for the maker’s internal design.

The table has these variables, each with its unit and its description, which summary (B) lists; lengths are in millimetres, loads in newtons:

designationthe designation, as SKF prints it
dbore diameter
Doutside diameter
Bwidth
rsmallest chamfer of the rings, r_{1,2} min
dasmallest diameter of a shaft shoulder, d_a min
Dalargest diameter of a housing shoulder, D_a max
ralargest fillet radius at a shoulder, r_a max
Cbasic dynamic load rating
C0basic static load rating
nlimlimiting speed, revolutions per minute
massmass, in kilograms
sourcethe catalogue page the values were read from

The catalogue

The open bearings of the series 618, 619, 60 and 62, bores 4 to 380 mm, transcribed from the product tables of the SKF catalogue Rolling bearings (PUB BU/P1 17000 EN), section 1.1, pages 260 to 302. Where SKF lists a bearing only with a brass cage, such as '61930 MA', that is the one given. The boundary dimensions follow ISO 15 and hold for any maker; the load ratings and the speed are SKF’s, and another maker’s differ.

See also: parts.screw.cap, parts.dowel, parts.key
