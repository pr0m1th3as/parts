Function Reference: parts.ring.retaining

parts: C = parts.ring.retaining ('data')

parts: C = parts.ring.retaining ('data', D1)

parts: S = parts.ring.retaining ('solid', D1)

parts: … = parts.ring.retaining (…, Name, Value)

Retaining rings, with the grooves they sit in: for shafts, DIN 471, and for bores, DIN 472, each of the normal and the heavy type.

C = parts.ring.retaining ('data') returns every retaining ring in the catalogue as a struct array, one entry for each maker’s article in each size, sorted by diameter. C = parts.ring.retaining ('data', D1) returns those for a shaft or bore of diameter D1: every article sold as that ring, each with its material and finish, and the groove to cut for it. Nothing is built.

S = parts.ring.retaining ('solid', D1) builds the ring as a solid.Shape whether or not anyone sells it, in any size its standard defines, free, as it is before fitting: lying on the xy plane, s thick, its axis on the z axis and its gap on the positive x axis. S.Name holds the designation.

Name/Value pairs:

'Bore'
true for a ring for a bore, DIN 472, false for one for a shaft, DIN 471, the default for 'solid'. For 'data' it keeps that kind only; without it both come.
'Heavy'
true for the heavy type, false for the normal one, the default for 'solid'. For 'data' it keeps that type only; without it both come.
'Maker'
For 'data': only that maker’s articles, such as 'REYHER', in any case.
'Detailed'
For 'solid': true builds the ring’s form: the section narrowing from b opposite the gap to b/2 at the free ends, the two lugs a high across the ring with a hole of diameter d5 in each, outwards for a shaft and inwards for a bore, and the gap between them as wide as a hole. No source dimensions the taper, the lugs or the gap, which the standards leave to the maker, so these are a modelling choice. By default (false) the ring is a flat ring b wide on d3, without a gap.

Each entry is a struct with these fields, lengths in millimetres:

designationthe designation of the standard, as in 'Retaining ring DIN 471 - 50 x 2'
standard'DIN 471' or 'DIN 472'
type'normal' or 'heavy'
d1diameter of the shaft or the bore
sthickness of the ring, nominal
d3diameter of the free ring, nominal: inside for a shaft, outside for a bore
aradial height of the lugs, largest
bradial width of the ring opposite the gap, about
d5diameter of the holes in the lugs, smallest
d4diameter the fitted ring sweeps, the room it needs: outside for a shaft, inside for a bore; NaN where the sources give none
d2diameter of the groove, nominal
mwidth of the groove, smallest (H13)
tdepth of the groove
ndistance from the groove to the end of the shaft or bore, smallest
material'spring steel' or a stainless steel by its number, as '1.4122'
finishthe finish as the maker names it, as 'plain' or 'zinc flake'
makerthe maker
articlethe maker’s article number
sourcethe catalogue and page the article was read from

The catalogue

Articles from REYHER, The Catalogue, edition 2020.04, English, pages 89 to 91 (spring steel) and 469 to 470 (stainless steel): every article of the two standards in sizes they define. Left out: the intermediate sizes REYHER sells outside the standards’ tables.

Dimensions of the rings and grooves from the tables of DIN 471:2011-04 and DIN 472:2011-10 as fasten.it reproduces them by permission of DIN, which set the sizes, read against the catalogues of Seeger-Orbis (2007) and Rotor Clip (2011) and REYHER’s Technical Information (2019, TI-91 to 97): every value but d4 in at least two of them. d4 is REYHER’s, a second source agreeing at about thirty sizes. DIN 471 and DIN 472 are current in their editions of 2026-05; DIN 472’s revises its tables in ways not read.
