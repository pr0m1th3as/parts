0.1.0 2026-10-14

Standard machine parts in the sizes their makers' catalogues list: deep groove ball bearings; socket head cap, low head cap, countersunk, raised countersunk, button, pan, cheese, hexagon head, shoulder and set screws; hexagon and flange nuts; plain washers; retaining rings; heat-set threaded inserts; dowel pins; and parallel keys. Each part is given as data for a calculation and as a solid for an assembly, every value cited to the catalogue or standard it comes from. The properties of engineering materials come with them.
